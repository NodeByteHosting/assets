# NodeByte Hosting: Partnership Rules, Requirements, & Benefits

This document outlines the eligibility criteria, operational rules, and performance metrics for the NodeByte Hosting Partnership Program, alongside the corresponding infrastructure and support benefits provided by NodeByte.

---

## 1. Content Creators & Streamers
This track is designed for creators who actively engage an audience through video production and live broadcasting.

### Minimum Requirements
* **YouTube:** 3,000 to 5,000 subscribers with an average of 1,500+ organic views within 14 days of an upload. Must maintain a schedule of at least 2 videos per month.
* **Twitch / Live Streaming:** 1,000+ followers with a consistent average of 20+ concurrent live viewers.
* **Short-Form (TikTok/Shorts):** 15,000+ followers with steady baseline engagement metrics (not isolated to a single viral clip).

### Partnership Rules
* **Above-the-Fold Branding:** NodeByte branding, a custom tracking URL, or an assigned promotional code must remain above the fold in video descriptions, stream panels, or bio links.
* **Integration Commitments:** A minimum of one dedicated shout-out, video integration, or product mention every 30 to 45 days.
* **The Anti-Ghosting Clause:** Failure to stream or upload for 45 consecutive days without prior written notice results in automatic suspension of partnership perks and discount tiers.

### What You Get From NodeByte (Performance-Based)
* **High-Tier Affiliate Structure:** A personalized tracking link and audience promo code granting a high-percentage cash payout or infrastructure credit match on every successful conversion.
* **Co-Branded Infrastructure Subsidies:** Access to dedicated partner-only hardware tiers at cost margin, fully offset once the partner hits baseline monthly referral targets.
* **Media & Graphics Package:** Access to official NodeByte transparent badges, stream overlays, and video bumper assets to integrate smoothly into production layouts.

## 2. Communities & Server Networks
This track covers established game networks (Minecraft, FiveM, RedM, etc.) requiring high-performance infrastructure to host public player bases.

### Minimum Requirements
* **Active Player Base:** 
  * *General Game Networks:* 50–100 concurrent players during peak hours.
  * *Roleplay Networks (FiveM/RedM):* An established community with a populated queue during peak times, or a minimum of 1,500 Discord members.
* **Project Longevity:** The community must have been actively running and managed for at least 3 to 6 months. NodeByte does not partner with unlaunched concepts.
* **Independent Management:** The community must maintain an internal staff and support structure to handle player-side issues. NodeByte support handles infrastructure, not in-game user management.

### Partnership Rules
* **Infrastructure Isolation:** Sponsored resources are strictly for running the community's public game servers, development nodes, or databases. Subleasing or reselling resources to third parties is grounds for immediate termination.
* **Mandatory Placement:** High-visibility "Powered by NodeByte" text/logo placement is required on server connect screens, automated in-game chat messages, and a dedicated `#partners` channel in Discord.
* **Co-Branded Incentives:** Partners must actively promote an exclusive community discount code to funnel their player base toward NodeByte for private hosting needs.

### What You Get From NodeByte (Value-Exchange)
* **Subsidized Enterprise Hardware:** Access to high-performance, dedicated-thread VDS allocation or bare-metal nodes at a strictly managed, discounted partner rate. **NodeByte does not provide 100% free production hardware.**
* **Bill-Offsetting Credits:** Ability to completely wipe out their monthly infrastructure invoice via conversion milestones (e.g., every $X generated via their community discount code earns $Y in direct infrastructure credits).
* **Advanced DDoS Mitigation:** Enterprise-grade edge protection configured to handle game-specific attack vectors, included at no extra cost to protect our network integrity.

## 3. Mod, Plugin, & Tool Developers
This track builds developer sentiment by supporting the creators who build frameworks, performance optimization tools, and ecosystem utilities.

### Minimum Requirements
* **Resource/Mod Authors:** A minimum of 25,000+ total downloads across verified platforms (GitHub, Modrinth, CurseForge, Tebex, etc.).
* **Discord Bot Developers:** The bot must be verified (where applicable) and actively serving at least 15,000+ active users or a substantial, verified count of guilds. Free/hobby bots must be open-source.
* **Active Maintenance:** The project repository or codebase must have recorded updates or commits within the last 60 days.

### Partnership Rules
* **Repository & Docs Attribution:** NodeByte must be explicitly credited as the official infrastructure sponsor at the top of the project's GitHub `README.md`, official documentation sites, or via the bot's `/info` command.
* **Telemetry & Feedback:** Partners agree to provide occasional engineering feedback, performance logs, or telemetry when running their software on NodeByte's latest node builds or artifacts.

### What You Get From NodeByte (Utility-Exchange)
* **Compute Allocation for Development:** Provision of strict, low-overhead development environments (low-spec VPS or database instances) solely for compilation, testing, and continuous integration pipelines. **Production/commercial hosting requires a standard paid plan.**
* **Alpha Network Access:** First-look access to test new network locations, hardware configurations, or internal panel tools before public rollout.
* **Direct Marketplace/Client Exposure:** Placement within the NodeByte partner directory or client panel, promoting their tool/plugin directly to our paid customer base looking for server optimizations, helping drive their own premium/Tebex sales.

## 4. General Global Program Rules
These rules apply universally to all tracks to protect network reputation, infrastructure overhead, and support operations.

* **Support Boundaries:** Partners receive priority infrastructure support via a dedicated Discord channel or internal ticket tier. NodeByte technicians do not write custom code, debug third-party game scripts, or repair broken database schemas. 
* **Accountability & Conduct:** Zero-tolerance policy for malicious activity (e.g., unannounced stress testing, network scanning, or hosting compromised content). Any violation of the standard Terms of Service (ToS) immediately voids the partnership.
* **Zero-Cost Protection:** NodeByte reserves the right to review partnership metrics monthly. If a partner’s conversion metrics or promotional output drop below the agreed-upon threshold, the account scales back to standard retail pricing with 14 days' notice.
